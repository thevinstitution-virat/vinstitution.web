=== WHO WE ARE ===
Vinstitution is an education-technology company based in New Delhi, India.
ISO 9001:2015 certified.
Motto: Uttiṣṭhata · Jāgrata · Prāpya (उत्तिष्ठत · जाग्रत · प्राप्य) — Arise. Awake. Attain.
We build four connected platforms for Indian institutions and learners. Each one
solves a problem completely on its own, and they share an identity, a data model
and a design language. An institution can adopt one, or run the whole stack.

=== THE FOUR PLATFORMS ===

1. VIDYAVERSE — the campus operating system
   Live at: vidyaverse.vinstitution.com
   The operating system for modern institutions. 47 modules behind a single
   login, all writing to one shared record: academics, fees, attendance,
   transport, hostel, documents, admissions and intelligence.
   Module categories: Academics (10), Operations (6), Finance (5),
   Communication (5), Document Studio (8), Intelligence (6).
   Notable: AI-powered WhatsApp updates to every parent — attendance, fees and
   results the moment they happen. Document Studio generates ID cards,
   marksheets, hall tickets and transfer certificates in one click.
   Admissions CRM. DPDP-ready.
   Vidyaverse is ALSO the identity provider for the ecosystem: it issues
   Ed25519-signed OIDC tokens, so roles and memberships carry across to the
   other platforms. Sign in once, step into the library and the tutor.

2. PDLMS PRO — the AI digital library
   Live at: pdlms.vinstitution.com
   A multi-tenant digital library for institutions. Four ways to read one book:
   reflowable EPUB, page-accurate PDF, natural text-to-speech audio, and Varta.
   Varta is the study assistant that answers questions from the institution's
   own library and cites the exact paragraph it used — not the whole book.
   Sanchika is the notebook where answers and highlights are saved, searchable
   later. Reading position syncs across every signed-in device.
   Enterprise: multi-tenant by design, branch hierarchy, five roles with least
   privilege. Signs in from the Vidyaverse campus dashboard.

3. DIGI CLASSROOM — the AI tutor that shows its sources
   Live at: dgcl.vinstitution.com
   An NCERT-grounded AI tutor for CBSE and ICSE learners, Classes 6–12.
   Runs on Sarvagya, our agentic-RAG engine. Every answer is grounded in NCERT
   and comes with its citation and a Bloom's-taxonomy level, so a student and a
   teacher can both see where it came from.
   Includes an adaptive practice-test engine and offline-ready study tools.
   Note: the browser tab may still read "Virat Gyankosh" — same product.

4. E-LEARNING PRACTEST — CBT mocks for government exams
   Live at: practest.live   (NOT practest.in — that domain does not work)
   Server-timed computer-based-test mocks that replicate the real exam hall:
   the exact question palette, section locks and negative marking of
   SSC, Banking (IBPS, SBI), Railways (RRB), UPSC and State PCS papers.
   Plus a video course library and analytics that show where marks leak.
   Practest is the consumer-facing arm — for individual aspirants and coaching
   institutes — and is hosted separately from the campus platforms.

=== HOW THEY FIT TOGETHER ===
Vidyaverse is the hub and the identity provider. PDLMS Pro and Digi Classroom
sign in from it over OIDC. Practest is standalone, built by the same team and
sharing the same assessment engine.

=== WHAT WE DECIDED EARLY (AND WON'T WALK BACK) ===
- AI that cites, or stays quiet. Answers carry their source or they are not given.
- Multi-tenant by architecture, not by configuration.
- Built for Bharat, not ported to it.
- One identity across four products.
- DPDP-ready from day one.
- ISO 9001:2015 certified.
