The reading experience

Four ways to read one book.

Upload once. Every title instantly becomes a reflowable EPUB, a page-accurate PDF, a natural-voice audiobook, and an AI you can talk to. Switch modes anytime — highlights, bookmarks and progress sync across all four.

EPUB

Reflowable, on any screen

Font size, sepia mode and a clean reading column that respects the publisher's layout. Distraction-free by design, on a laptop or a ₹8,000 phone.

PDF

Page-accurate, citation-ready

The exact page a lecturer refers to, with the exact numbering. Every Varta citation links back to a page and paragraph a student can verify by eye.

Audiobook

Natural text-to-speech

Every book becomes listenable. Study at the desk, revise on the bus — progress carries back to the text when they open it again.

Varta

The book that answers back.

Most AI guesses. Varta reads your textbook and answers only from it — with a citation you can verify on the page. If it isn't in the book, Varta says so.

Trust

Paragraph-level citations

Every claim links back to the exact page and paragraph in the source book, so a student — or an examiner — can check it in seconds.

Recall

Instant flashcards

Turn any explanation into spaced-repetition cards in one tap, without leaving the page they were reading.

Practice

Exam-style practice questions

Generate questions from the chapter in front of them, pitched at the material actually covered rather than a generic bank.

Sanchika

A study archive that builds itself

Highlights, Varta explanations, annotations and flashcards collect automatically across every book — one evolving notebook to revise from.

PDF Studio

A full annotation studio in the browser

Freehand ink, smart shapes, highlights, redaction, OCR search and audio notes — Drawboard-grade tools with no download, syncing to Sanchika across devices.

For faculty

See what they read. Know what they skip.

Assign books to batches, then read the struggling-chapter heatmap: which pages take longest, which chapters trigger the most Varta questions, who is falling behind.

Secure for Bharat

Enterprise-grade. Built for institutions.

From a single coaching centre to a multi-campus university, the same architecture scales without ever mixing one institution's data with another's.

Isolation

Multi-tenant by design

Every institution's data lives in its own logical boundary. One breach can never cross tenants — the separation is structural, not a query filter.

Structure

Branch hierarchy

Model campuses, branches and departments, and assign books and seats per branch rather than flattening a university into one list.

Access

Five roles, least privilege

Super Admin, Admin, Librarian, Teacher and Student — across both institutional (B2B) and independent (B2C) accounts, with SSO from Vidyaverse.
