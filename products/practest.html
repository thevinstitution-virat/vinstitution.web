Built like the real thing

Every detail of the exam hall, replicated.

Engineered for SSC CGL, SBI PO, SBI Clerk, IBPS, Railways RRB, UPSC and State PCS aspirants across India.

The palette

Five states, exactly as SSC & IBPS render them

Answered, not answered, marked for review, answered & marked, not visited — the same colours, the same teardrop shape, the same section locks. When you sit the real CBT, nothing surprises you.

The clock

A timer the browser cannot cheat

Timing is server-authoritative to within a second, and auto-submit fires on the server when time expires — even if the tab is closed or the laptop dies.

Resilience

Crash-safe by default

Every answer autosaves as it is marked. Internet drops mid-section, and the attempt resumes exactly where it stopped — with the clock still honest.

Analytics

Analysis that names the problem

Every submission is re-scored on the server from raw answers: accuracy, time-per-question, subject and topic breakdowns, batch percentile and rank — recomputed, never estimated.

Courses

Video courses with resume-anywhere

Modules and lessons in exam order with watched-seconds logged automatically. Close the tab mid-lecture and the player reopens within seconds of where you stopped, on any device.

Institutes

Batches and activation codes

Coaching institutes issue activation codes that unlock a batch instantly, with all-India rank and percentile computed across the cohort.

How it works

From sign-up to scorecard in three steps.

Register & verify

Create a free account and verify email and mobile with an OTP — about two minutes.

Activate your batch

Enter the activation code from your institute, or pay online for instant access when enrolment is open.

Practise & analyse

Attempt timed mocks, watch structured lessons, and track accuracy, percentile and rank after every test.
