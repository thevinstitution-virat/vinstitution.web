The platform

Everything a serious learner needs, in one place.

Cutting-edge retrieval and assessment powering a personalised, trustworthy learning experience.

Agentic RAG

AI tutoring system

Conversational AI with role-based answers, CBSE/ICSE alignment and citation-backed retrieval over the NCERT books themselves — not a scraped approximation of them.

Smart testing

Practest engine

Adaptive assessment with intelligent question generation and real-time, chapter-level performance analytics that point at the specific gap.

Organised

Structured question bank

A clean Board → Class → Subject → Chapter hierarchy with metadata tagging, so a question is always findable and always in the right context.

Adaptive

Computer-adaptive testing

CAT algorithms move difficulty up and down with performance, so a learner spends their time at the edge of what they know rather than repeating what they've mastered.

Deep insight

Bloom's-taxonomy analytics

Mastery tracked by cognitive level, with honest strength and gap reporting — remembering a definition and applying it are scored as the different skills they are.

Universal

Multi-modal learning

Visual, reading and practice modes so every learner meets the material in the way they actually think best.

How it works

From doubt to mastery in three steps.

Ask anything

Type a doubt in Hindi or English from any NCERT chapter, Classes 6–12.

Learn with proof

Every answer cites the exact textbook page and tags its Bloom's level, so the learner can check it and the teacher can trust it.

Practise adaptively

Practest tunes difficulty to the learner and tracks mastery until it sticks.

Beyond the tutor

The productivity suite.

A learning app should protect focus and motivation, not just serve content. These tools — built for Indian students — do exactly that.

Gamified active recall

FlashBharat

Indian-context flashcards, live quiz battles and cultural badges that make revision something a student actually returns to.

Offline-first

OfflineOrbit

Download lessons, tests and notes, study with zero internet, and sync smartly on reconnect — because connectivity is not a given.

Smart focus mode

FocusShield

Blocks distracting apps during study, with a whitelist the student controls and an emergency escape so it never becomes a trap.

AI study coach

MoodMentor

Tracks how a learner is feeling and adapts the schedule — coaching that protects motivation, not just marks.

Parent & teacher view

ParentPulse

Progress insight and direct messaging so families stay in the loop without the nagging.

Syllabus-aware pomodoro

CurricuTimer

Adaptive study timers tuned to the grade and the CBSE/ICSE syllabus, with engagement tracked over the term. Plus Mitram, a focus companion that runs quick attention checks.
