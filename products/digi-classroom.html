Beyond the tutor

The productivity suite.

A learning app should protect focus and motivation, not just serve content. These tools — built for Indian students — do exactly that.

Gamified active recall

FlashBharat

Indian-context flashcards, live quiz battles and cultural badges that make revision something a student actually returns to.

Offline-first

OfflineOrbit

Download lessons, tests and notes, study with zero internet, and sync smartly on reconnect — because connectivity is not a given.

Smart focus mode

FocusShield

Blocks distracting apps during study, with a whitelist the student controls and an emergency escape so it never becomes a trap.

AI study coach

MoodMentor

Tracks how a learner is feeling and adapts the schedule — coaching that protects motivation, not just marks.

Parent & teacher view

ParentPulse

Progress insight and direct messaging so families stay in the loop without the nagging.

Syllabus-aware pomodoro

CurricuTimer

Adaptive study timers tuned to the grade and the CBSE/ICSE syllabus, with engagement tracked over the term. Plus Mitram, a focus companion that runs quick attention checks.
