What makes it different

The parts institutions actually feel.

The differentiator

Every parent reached, on WhatsApp

Attendance, fees and results the moment they happen — with an AI that answers inbound questions in Hindi or English from live student data, day and night. Razorpay links sit inside the chat and the books reconcile themselves.

Document Studio

Every document, generated in one click

Pick a type — ID card, marksheet, hall ticket, transfer certificate — and Vidyaverse renders it from your institution's template, branding and live student data. Ready to print or send, in bulk.

Group photos

AI face mapping for photo day

Upload one group photo; face detection extracts each student and assigns them to the right profile. A three-day workflow collapses into about twenty minutes.

One record

A dashboard that is always live

Every module writes to one shared record, so attendance, fees and admissions are never a stale export. Nothing is re-entered, and nothing drifts out of sync between departments.

Admissions

Built for the two-week rush

Bulk CSV import with approval workflows and data-status tracking, so five hundred admissions in a fortnight don't become five hundred manual entries — and ID cards follow in minutes.

Governance

Roles, consent and audit trails

Super Admin, Admin, Teacher and Student roles with least-privilege defaults. Parent messaging is consent-first with honoured opt-outs, and access is audit-logged throughout.

How it works

From sign-up to a running campus in a day.

Set up your institution

Add classes, sections and students — or bulk-import from a spreadsheet. Your branding is applied everywhere automatically.

Toggle the modules you need

Start with attendance and fees; add transport or hostel later. Every module shares one database, so nothing is re-entered.

Go live with parents

Connect WhatsApp and updates start flowing. Generate ID cards, marksheets and certificates on demand.
