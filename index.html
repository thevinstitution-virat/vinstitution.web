Why Vinstitution

Decisions we made early, and refuse to walk back.

Education software fails quietly — a wrong AI answer, a leaked record, a parent who never got the message. These are the commitments that keep that from happening.

AI that cites, or stays quiet

Varta and Sarvagya answer from your textbook and link the exact page and paragraph. If it isn't in the source, the system says so instead of inventing it.

Multi-tenant by architecture

Every institution's data sits in its own logical boundary with least-privilege roles. Isolation is structural, not a filter someone remembered to apply.

Built for Bharat, not ported to it

Hindi and English throughout, WhatsApp as the parent channel, offline-first study tools, and NCERT as the source of truth — because that is how Indian institutions actually work.

One identity, four products

Students and staff carry a single institutional login across the campus OS, the library and the tutor. No parallel user lists drifting out of sync.

DPDP-ready from day one

Consent-first messaging, honoured opt-outs, audit-logged access, encryption in transit and at rest. Minors' data is treated as the sensitive category it is.

ISO 9001:2015 certified

Documented processes behind the product, not just the pitch — from onboarding and support to how we handle a change request mid-term.
